# Answering a system design problem

[← Start here](../README.md)

1. Read the unchanged [System Design Interviews](../sources/karan/README.md#system-design-interviews) lesson.
2. Read Ashish’s [How to Answer a System Design Interview Problem](https://algomaster.io/learn/system-design-interviews/answering-framework).
3. Use the [design worksheet](../templates/DESIGN.md) before reading any solution.

For a 45-minute practice session, try 5 minutes clarifying requirements, 5 estimating scale, 10 on APIs/data and a high-level diagram, 15 on one bottleneck or failure, and 10 discussing tradeoffs and revising. Adjust the split to the problem.

Ask what must work, at what scale, how quickly, and under which failures. Explain why each component exists. A reasonable design with clear assumptions is more useful than a diagram copied from memory.
