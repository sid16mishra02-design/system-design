# System design interview problem bank

[← Start here](../README.md) · [Design worksheet](../templates/DESIGN.md)

All problem labels, links, and source difficulty groups below are preserved from Ashish’s list. They are optional practice after the scheduled five case studies. Difficulty is the source’s classification, not a prerequisite guarantee; parking garages and vending machines may emphasize object-oriented design.

Known source mismatch: “Design Autocomplete for Search Engines” points to an Instagram lesson. That link is preserved for traceability; skip it when choosing an autocomplete solution. Some providers may require sign-in or payment.
